Want a proper trade? Learn roofing on real jobs, from people who do it every day.

We are an established roofing firm and we are looking for someone who wants to get into the trade. No experience needed. What we need is somebody who turns up, works hard, and wants to learn. It is outdoor work, in all weather, and it is not easy. If that does not put you off, read on.

Who we are after

We are not hiring knowledge. We are hiring attitude.

You do not need to know anything about roofing. We will teach you. What we cannot teach is turning up.

This is for you if

  • You want a real trade, not just a job
  • You are happy working outdoors, whatever the weather
  • Heights do not bother you
  • You will be there every day, on time
  • [Driving licence needed or not, to be confirmed]

Probably not for you if

  • You want an easy job or a desk job
  • You expect top money before you have learned the trade
  • Early mornings are a problem
